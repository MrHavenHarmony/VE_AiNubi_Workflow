# Rain & Thunder — 100-Scene Storyboard

A 16:9 cinematic photorealistic rain film. Each numbered item is planned as one distinct 10-second scene, for a total target duration of 16 minutes 40 seconds. Keep the imagery calm and immersive, with natural rain, wind, water, and occasional distant thunder only. No music, voices, narration, people, text, or watermark.

## Workflow settings

- VideoExpress: Landscape 16:9
- Image style: Photorealistic (Cinematic)
- Advanced Mode: Manual Video Length = 10 seconds
- Audio prompt: natural environmental rain/thunder ambience; keep Video Only off
- Public Gallery: off
- Submit in batches of five; wait for capacity before starting the next batch.
- Make every image prompt and camera move specific to its scene.

The first 11 scene requests have been submitted during this run. Scenes 1–4 used generated stills with image-to-video prompts; scenes 5–11 used direct text-to-video. Submission does not mean the clips have finished rendering. Scenes 72–100 remain storyboard prompts awaiting generation.
