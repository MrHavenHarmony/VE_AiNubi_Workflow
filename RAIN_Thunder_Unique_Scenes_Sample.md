# VideoExpress Rain & Thunder — Unique Scene Sample

A five-scene sample from the ongoing cinematic rain-and-thunder project.

## Workflow settings

- Format: 16:9 landscape
- Look: cinematic, photorealistic
- Creative mode: enabled
- Clip length: 10 seconds per scene
- Audio: natural rain, water, and distant thunder only; no narration, voices, or music
- Scene design: distinct locations, compositions, and camera movement
- Status: these prompts were submitted as scenes 16–20; this sample documents the requested scenes and does not claim they have been added to the final timeline or verified in the exported movie.

## Scenes 16–20

### 16. Desert roadside diner in a cloudburst
A cinematic ground-level slow dolly approaches an abandoned roadside diner on a remote salt flat during a rare desert cloudburst. Rain ripples across shallow pools and the diner’s faded neon glow reflects in the wet ground; the camera gently tilts toward distant lightning behind the storm clouds. Photorealistic, 16:9, 10 seconds. Natural rain and distant thunder only.

### 17. Alpine cable car above a glacial ravine
A cinematic telephoto tracking shot follows an empty, weathered red cable car gliding above a deep glacial ravine in wind-driven rain. It passes dark pines and misty rock walls before the camera widens to reveal a brief lightning glow across the mountain peaks. Photorealistic, 16:9, 10 seconds. Rain, mountain wind, and distant thunder only.

### 18. Tropical shipwreck during monsoon
A cinematic water-level glide passes the timbers of an old wooden shipwreck above a living tropical reef during a monsoon. Rain stipples the sea; luminous fish dart between coral as the camera slowly arcs toward a storm-veiled island and distant lightning. Photorealistic, 16:9, 10 seconds. Rain, surf, and distant thunder only.

### 19. Cedar forest shrine
A cinematic stabilized glide follows a moss-covered lantern path through an ancient cedar forest toward a small weathered shrine. Rain beads on fern fronds, droplets fall from the canopy, and fine mist drifts between tree trunks as the camera gently rises. Photorealistic, 16:9, 10 seconds. Natural rainfall, leaf drips, and distant thunder only.

### 20. Hydroelectric spillway in a thunderstorm
A cinematic low forward dolly crosses a wet maintenance walkway inside an immense hydroelectric dam spillway, revealing torrents of water descending through concrete channels. The camera tilts toward the open storm sky as lightning briefly illuminates the spray. Photorealistic, 16:9, 10 seconds. Water roar, rain, and muffled distant thunder only.
