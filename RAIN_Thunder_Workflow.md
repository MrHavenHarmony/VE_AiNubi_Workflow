# Rain & Thunder Film — VideoExpress Workflow

A calm, cinematic rain film made from distinct 10-second landscape clips. This file records the setup and sample prompts for the current project.

## Settings

- Aspect ratio: Landscape 16:9
- Image type: Photorealistic (Cinematic)
- Clip length: 10 seconds (Advanced Mode → Manual Video Length)
- Visual approach: distinct location, composition, and camera movement for each clip
- Audio: natural rain, wind, water, and occasional distant thunder only
- Exclude: music, voices, narration, text, and people
- Public gallery: off

## Workflow

1. Open **Create Video From Prompt** and choose **Landscape 16:9**.
2. Select **Photorealistic (Cinematic)**.
3. Write a unique image prompt describing the scene and composition.
4. Write a matching 10-second motion and ambient-audio prompt.
5. In **Advanced Mode**, enable **Manual Video Length** and set the slider to **10 seconds**. Keep **Video Only (No Sound)** off so the natural rain ambience can be generated.
6. Generate the image, review it, then create the video.
7. Repeat with a new setting and camera move each time. Queue no more than five videos in parallel, then check the Media Library for completed clips.

## Scene prompt samples

### 01 — Alpine cabin over a rain-dark lake
**Image:** Cinematic photorealistic 16:9 wide landscape. A secluded timber cabin at the edge of a misty alpine lake during gentle night rain. Warm amber windows reflect on rippling dark water; pine silhouettes frame the foreground and a distant mountain ridge fades into mist. Natural realistic textures, calm mood, no people, no text.

**Motion and audio:** One continuous 10-second aerial drift above the rain-dark lake toward the warmly lit cabin. Fine rain stipples the water, mist moves between pines, and a distant storm cloud softly flickers. Natural rain patter and one low distant thunder roll only; no music, voice, or narration.

### 02 — Rain on the cabin window
**Image:** Photorealistic cinematic 16:9 night interior. A timber cabin window covered in detailed raindrops, a soft amber table lamp and folded blanket in the foreground, deep blue pine forest and distant storm clouds beyond the glass. Intimate, peaceful mood; no people, no text.

**Motion and audio:** One continuous 10-second slow slider move along the rain-streaked glass, shifting focus from water beads to the golden lamp reflection and shadowy forest. A brief distant lightning glow softly reveals the clouds. Gentle rain on glass and roof with one low distant thunder roll only; no music, voice, or narration.

### 03 — Cedar forest stream
**Image:** Cinematic photorealistic 16:9 wilderness scene in a dense cedar forest during steady summer rain. Giant mossy trunks, fern fronds, silver rain threads, and a shallow stream winding over dark stones. Soft overcast light and drifting mist; no cabin, no people, no text.

**Motion and audio:** One continuous 10-second low tracking shot beside the stream through the rain-soaked cedar forest. Droplets fall from fern tips, water curls around stones, and mist drifts between trunks. Immersive rainfall and stream ambience with soft distant thunder only; no music, voice, or narration.

### 04 — Lighthouse on a rugged coast
**Image:** Photorealistic cinematic 16:9 wide view of a lonely stone lighthouse on a rugged black-rock coastline during a rainstorm at blue hour. White surf bursts against the rocks, rain sweeps diagonally across frame, and the lantern casts a warm amber beam through sea mist. No people, no text.

**Motion and audio:** One continuous 10-second slow push toward the lighthouse from wet coastal rocks. Heavy rain streams past the lens, waves crash and recede, and the rotating lantern beam cuts through salt mist. Muted distant lightning glow; natural rain, wind, surf, and low thunder only; no music, voice, or narration.
